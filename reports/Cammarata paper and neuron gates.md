# Tiny adders supply Cammarata's missing ground truth

Nick Cammarata's "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" (Goodfire, 15 October 2026) and this project mostly agree. The disagreement that looks sharpest turns out to be miscast. Both find that trained networks are legible in their own variables and illegible only when read in someone else's. This project's 1-layer addition transformers contain **no carry neuron (best unit R² 0.30–0.40)**. Yet in four of five seeds, **every MLP unit that cleanly fits the sum's inputs is a count threshold** (OR3, majority, AND3), which is the textbook depth-2 threshold circuit for parity. That is the same move by which Cammarata turns InceptionV1's "cat/car" neuron into a V-shape detector. The carry itself is a direction in the residual stream, a basis his neuron-level claims do not cover. The project adds something the essay lacks: an electrical meaning of "gate". Under the static discipline, a neuron is a reusable gate only if its gain at threshold exceeds 1, which takes a training margin of 4. By that standard every learned structure the project measured is far from gate-like: margins of **0.4–3.5 standard deviations, against 104 for a trained gate neuron**, even where the structure is semantically clean. The bridge runs through six links, and the last one is what the essay says large models cannot supply: full training checkpoints of a network whose algorithm is known. The proposed experiment, "squeeze, watch, snap", retrains the project's adder at MLP widths from 256 down to 4, with dense checkpoints and a random-truth-table positive control. Cammarata predicts coarser neurons that still do one job each. Superposition predicts unrelated jobs packed onto shared neurons once the width falls below about 27 units. The experiment needs a few hours of CPU time and mostly existing code. Its first stage needs no training at all: seed B:3 was trained with the same architecture and hyperparameters as four clean seeds, yet has no clean units.

## Both programs treat neurons as gates, but grade them on different scales

Cammarata's essay is a lab-published "working hypothesis", not a peer-reviewed paper. It argues that superposition, in the narrow sense of "unrelated things are placed together because they don't interfere", was the wrong explanation for polysemanticity ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/); [Goodfire research index](https://www.goodfire.com/research)). Its headline language-model figure is explicitly preliminary. In audits of Qwen3-32B, **roughly 90% of neuron poles have one main job, about 7% have two related jobs, and about 2–4% are not yet understood**, and the author says the split moves with thresholds ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The controlled evidence is in vision. InceptionV1's last layer goes from about 5% legible with 2020-era tools to about 50% with fixed tools, about 70% after retraining with modern regularization, and **about 99% in a ConvNeXt of the same size** ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). As of 28 October no code, data or companion paper had been released. Press coverage reports no independent replication and notes Goodfire's stake in the thesis. Neel Nanda called it useful for frontier models but not "a significant update to the big picture" ([Implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/goodfire-cammarata-ai-neurons-easier-to-read/)).

The shared premise is old. McCulloch and Pitts modelled the neuron as an all-or-none threshold element ([McCulloch & Pitts 1943](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02478259)). Cammarata now describes neurons less as "human-shaped nouns" and more as "control flow… often its job is to gate a region of the network", and adds that "the negative side tells us what distinction matters" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). His sharpest evidence is a Santa ablation in Qwen3-32B. **Removing the eight neurons with a Santa negative side drops P(Santa) to 6%, while removing the twenty neurons labelled Christmas or Santa leaves 76%** ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/)). The project built gates as single neurons. One sigmoid neuron with three weights learns AND, OR, NAND or NOR in 100 of 100 seeds. XOR needs a hidden neuron plus a skip connection (100 of 100 seeds, against 57 of 100 for a 2→2→1 net), as the non-separability of parity requires ([MINIGATES_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_minigates/MINIGATES_REPORT.md); [Minsky & Papert](https://openlibrary.org/isbn/0262130432.json)).

The two programs grade gates on different scales. Cammarata's scale is semantic: does one explanation predict what drives the neuron? His "explanations that could lose" test is the most rigorous version. An agent reads only a neuron's explanation and writes new examples and near misses before anything is measured. **New examples fired their neurons strongly 76% of the time, near misses 11%, and the explanation won for 164 of 183 neurons.** Texts for a distant concept fired about 1% of the time ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The project's scale is electrical: the static discipline of digital design. It requires that "if VIN ≤ VIL then VOUT ≥ VOH", with noise margins between the thresholds ([MIT 6.111](https://web.mit.edu/6.111/www/f2005/tutprobs/digital_answers.html)). At ε = 0.05, **no 2-input gate trained to margin 3.5 passes, and every one trained to margin 4 does**. The cause is gain: a sigmoid's slope at threshold is |w|/4, so a margin-2 NOT gate has gain about 1 and outputs only 0.12/0.88, while a margin-4 gate has gain about 2 and restores ([MINIGATES_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_minigates/MINIGATES_REPORT.md)). Von Neumann's restoring organs are the classic argument for why only gates that tighten their inputs compose without error building up ([von Neumann 1956](https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400882618-003)).

The two scales touch at one point: weak activations. In a medium ConvNeXt, **removing each neuron's weakest 75% of activations moves accuracy from 72% to 71%. Removing the strongest 7%, matched for total activation, drops it to 4%** ([Essay figure](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/)). Downstream readers that ignore sub-threshold activity are what a noise margin formalizes. The static discipline asks for more than tolerance at one stage, though. It asks every stage to push signals back toward the rails, and that is the property the project found missing in learned structures. The table lays out the full chain, and the sections below take its links in turn.

| Link | Cammarata's claim | What this project found | Literature that joins them |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Neurons are threshold gates | Neurons are "verbs" that gate regions; negative poles carry the distinction | One sigmoid neuron is AND/OR/NAND/NOR; XOR needs two | McCulloch & Pitts; Minsky & Papert |
| 2. "Clean" has two meanings | One main job; explanations that could lose | Static discipline: margin 4, gain about 2, ε = 0.05 | MIT 6.111; von Neumann; mean-field signal propagation |
| 3. Claims depend on the basis | Neuron poles are clean; the LM residual stream is an open question | Carry is a residual direction; MLP units are count thresholds | Toy Models' privileged basis; linear representation hypothesis |
| 4. Apparent mess has causes | Bad tools, bad explanations, bad learning, recursive generalization, superposition | The first three appear, and were fixed, in the project's own history | Marks & Tegmark; Mu & Andreas; Minnick |
| 5. Readers set the interface | "Become another reader" | Transplants need matched statistics; the CPU head needs a result-check reward | Li & Janson's spoofing; RL-NTM |
| 6. Development decides | Watch a neuron grow; LLMs lack checkpoints | Full checkpoints, known algorithm, an anomalous seed | Leavitt & Morcos; pre-registration |

## The missing carry neuron is a bad explanation, not superposition

Cammarata lists five causes of apparent polysemanticity: "Bad tools… Bad explanations… Bad learning… Recursive generalization… Superposition". He insists superposition be "the last hypothesis" rather than the null ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The project's best-known negative result looks at first like evidence for the last cause. The pre-registered transformer study predicted a carry gate inside 1-layer addition transformers. It found that **the best MLP unit fits the carry truth table with R² of only 0.30–0.40**, and that no subset of up to 40 units reads the carry at 99%. A trained one-neuron carry gate fits it with R² 1.000, with its two levels 104 within-level standard deviations apart ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). The earlier bridging notes call this a counter-example candidate to the essay. It is not one, for two reasons.

The first reason is the basis. Toy Models of Superposition argues that an elementwise nonlinearity gives neurons a privileged basis, so features are expected to line up with neurons only where such a basis exists ([Elhage et al. 2022](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html)). (The notes took this argument from background knowledge rather than a re-opened page.) Cammarata's claims are about neuron poles. On residual streams in language models he says only "We don't know whether language models need a similar fix" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The project's causally used carry object sits in the residual stream at resid_mid. It is a label-free direction (PC1) whose interchange moves outputs in **97.4–100% of cases in family B and 88.3–98.9% in C2**, while the logistic-probe direction moves 0% ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). A direction in a non-privileged basis is exactly what the essay leaves open, so it cannot count against a claim about neurons.

The second reason is the variable. Inside the MLP, where the basis is privileged, the units are clean, just not about the carry. In seeds B:0, B:1, B:2 and B:4, the units whose best fit on (a_k, b_k, c_k) reaches R² ≥ 0.8 number **96, 100, 98 and 78, and 100% of them are count thresholds**: OR3 (25–40 per model), majority (31–41) and AND3 (14–31). This is XOR3 = [n ≥ 1] − [n ≥ 2] + [n ≥ 3] ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)), the depth-2 construction of symmetric functions from threshold elements that goes back to Minnick's 1961 magnetic-core logic ([Minnick 1961](https://doi.org/10.1109/TEC.1961.5219146); [Siu, Roychowdhury & Kailath 1995](https://openlibrary.org/isbn/0133007081.json)). The exploratory follow-up then showed that the residual direction is not "the carry" either. PC1 loads on a_k and b_k with coefficients **0.94–1.71 times its loading on c_k**. On cross pairs in family B it follows the source's *answer* 77.7–90.5% of the time and the source's carry only 9.5–31.3%. Writing PC1 to the training mean for (k, a_k, b_k, carry), instead of for carry alone, raises transplant success **from 54.8–57.7% (chance) to 100%** in four B models ([Follow-up SUMMARY](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_followup/SUMMARY.md)). The network computes a fused count and thresholds it. "Carry" is the engineer's noun for a quantity the network never isolates. This is the cat/car-to-V-shape correction at the smallest possible scale: a neuron looks messy because it was asked about the wrong variable.

The same arithmetic settles a question the earlier literature review left open: is the majority unit a carry neuron? The full-adder identity a + b + c = s + 2·c_out means **MAJ(a_k, b_k, c_k) is exactly the carry out of position k**. Parity is the count minus twice that majority. So the network does contain clean carry-out neurons in the engineer's frame. It uses them inside the parity rather than passing them on, because in a 1-layer model the next position recomputes its carry from the emitted digit. In the textbook construction the majority term enters with a negative sign. If the trained readout also writes MAJ units negatively onto s_k, they are inhibitory gates in exactly Cammarata's negative-pole sense. Nobody has checked the sign of those output weights yet. Kruthoff's full-adder mapping of a 2-layer addition model ([Kruthoff 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07993)) and Hänni et al.'s proof that Boolean gates in an MLP need not be one neuron per gate ([Hänni et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05451)) bracket this reading.

Read through Cammarata's taxonomy, the project's history has already hit three of his five causes and fixed each one. **Bad tools:** the first transformer report found trained and untrained models equally probe-readable and called probes uninformative. The later study probed at resid_mid and found 92–95% trained against 78–79% at random init ([TRANSFORMER_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer/TRANSFORMER_REPORT.md); [Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). The 0% causal effect of the logistic-probe direction is an extreme case of Marks & Tegmark's finding that logistic directions tilt away from causal ones ([Marks & Tegmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06824)). **Bad explanations:** carry versus count, above. **Bad learning or architecture:** tanh XOR nets settle on one circuit type in 67–77% of seeds, while ReLU nets split into 12 types with the dominant one at only 30–38% ([XOR REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results/REPORT.md)). The relative-position C2 family grows single-combination detectors such as ¬a ∧ b ∧ ¬c instead of thresholds ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). Each of those detectors still has one job, and architecture sets which kind of clean the network reaches. That mirrors Cammarata's InceptionV1-to-ConvNeXt jump at fixed size.

The remaining two causes, recursive generalization and superposition, have never been tested here, and seed B:3 is where they would show. B:3 matches the other B seeds in architecture, width, optimizer and step count, and adds 10-bit sums exactly. Like seeds 0, 2 and 4 it uses its seed for both initialization and data order. Yet **B:3 has no units with R² ≥ 0.8 on the sum's inputs, and needs 20 units for a 99% sum readout, against 3–4 in the other seeds** ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). The follow-up shows why. B:3's median unit η² rises from 0.25 to 0.84 once the bit position k is added as a grouping variable, so its units are position-specific ([Follow-up SUMMARY](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_followup/SUMMARY.md)). From the same table, a lenient proxy for "one main job" can be computed. This report computed it; the source reports did not. It is the share of active units whose activity the task variables plus position explain at η² ≥ 0.8: **80%, 80%, 86% and 88% for B:0, B:1, B:2 and B:4, and 54% for B:3**. The first four are strikingly close to Cammarata's ~90%. The proxy is generous, though, because any function of those variables counts. Whether B:3's position-specific units carry *the same* threshold across their positions, which would be recursive generalization along the position axis, or *unrelated* thresholds at unrelated positions, which would be packing, is precisely the "local versus non-local" test the essay proposes: "generalization should be local, superposition should be non-local" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)).

Universality supplies a caution for both sides. Cammarata reports the same pose neuron in DINOv2 and two ConvNeXts ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). Gurnee et al. find only **1–5% of GPT-2 neurons universal across seeds** by activation correlation ([Gurnee et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12181)). Chughtai et al. separate strong universality, the same circuit in every model, from weak, the same principles implemented differently ([Chughtai et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03025)). The project's XOR nets sit firmly in the weak camp: five circuit types for one function. Same-type circuits transplant directly at 96%, different-type ones at 14% ([XOR REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results/REPORT.md)). Cleanliness and universality are separate claims, and a test of the first should not assume the second.

## Clean neurons need not be restoring gates

The project's construction arc measured what semantic audits never measure: whether a clean part survives being chained. The snake game (37,521 gates) and the 8-bit CPU (4,352 gates) run **with zero wrong bits over 9,000–12,000 snake ticks and 15,332 CPU ticks at margin 4**, with the worst signal 0.030–0.031 from a rail ([SNAKE_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_snake/SNAKE_REPORT.md); [CPU_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_cpu/CPU_REPORT.md)). Margin-3 gates pass 400-layer random circuits and hold a latch, yet break the snake on tick 1. Its 256-input crash-detector OR tree amplifies an analog zero through **0.047, 0.086, 0.141, 0.258, 0.639 and 0.997 by level 5**. The report puts it plainly: "Random circuits rarely put a gate in its worst case for many layers in a row; real machines do it all the time" ([SNAKE_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_snake/SNAKE_REPORT.md)). The closest neural-network theory is mean-field signal propagation. There, the average gain χ₁ separates an ordered phase, where perturbations shrink, from a chaotic one, where they grow, and sets trainable depth ([Poole et al. 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05340); [Schoenholz et al. 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01232)). Differentiable logic-gate networks take the other route. They train relaxed gates and harden them afterwards, losing under 0.1% on MNIST, but train only to about 8–10 layers ([Petersen et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08277)). The literature searches behind this report found no source that applies the static discipline to sigmoid networks, so the margin-4 rule remains the project's own bridge.

On this electrical scale, everything the trained transformers learned is analog. The carry direction's gate margin is **0.41–0.70 standard deviations**. It rises only to 2.79–3.54 after each current-digit group's mean is removed, and to 0.91 in B:3. The best individual units score 1.4–2.1, against 104 for a trained carry neuron and 122–238 for library gates ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md); [Follow-up SUMMARY](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_followup/SUMMARY.md)). Yet B's analog carry gate, dropped into a 64-bit ripple adder whose sum bits are library XOR neurons, **adds 100% of 100,000 sums**, because the library gates downstream restore every bit ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). Restoration can be borrowed from neighbours. This lets a shallow network get by with semantically clean but electrically soft parts. It also means Cammarata's cleanliness percentages say nothing about whether clean neurons would survive being stacked deep without help. A neuron audit that reports "one main job" should add a column for gain or margin. The project's scale already spans 0.5 (raw residual direction), about 3 (context-corrected), and 104 (trained gate).

The project's most general lesson bears directly on the essay's program. Cammarata defines clean as "a network's parts are built to be read, and built upon, by its downstream readers", and sets interpretability the goal to "become another reader" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The project shows that readers are calibrated to a signal's statistics, not only its meaning. A transplanted XOR circuit works **56% of the time directly and 99% after matching its mean and spread**. A hand-built reference circuit goes from 27% to 100% under the same two-parameter alignment, while a sham circuit reaches only 10% ([XOR REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results/REPORT.md)). The exact carry, written in as a class mean, breaks every model (≤ 0.2%). Written as a context-conditioned mean, it works perfectly ([Follow-up SUMMARY](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_followup/SUMMARY.md)). Li & Janson call the failure mode "spoofing": a replacement value injects information inconsistent with the rest of the example ([Li & Janson 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09951)). The essay contains the same failure at scale without naming it. A sparse autoencoder that explains 82% of the variance of the squeezed InceptionV1 layer **drops top-1 from 58.8% to 43.0% when spliced in** ([Essay figure](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/)), just as a class mean that is right on average breaks the adder. Neuron-level attribution graphs succeed partly because they read the network in its own coordinates and statistics. External dictionaries have to earn that alignment, and Sutter et al. warn that a map powerful enough to align anything proves nothing ([Sutter et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08802)).

## A frozen gate CPU turns circuit ecology into a measurable race

The CPU-GPT is the cleanest component the essay could ask for. It is a 2-layer GPT whose fourth attention head is the frozen 4,389-neuron gate CPU, with every neuron a margin-4 gate with a known job. The GPT has to learn to read it. Without the computer, a same-size GPT scores **96–100% on training pairs but 1.9–18.4% on held-out operands**, which is memorization. With the head and a reward for the computer's own correct result, seed 0 reaches 100% on held-out addition, subtraction and multiplication. Switching the head off drops held-out addition to 0% ([CPUGPT_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_cpugpt/CPUGPT_REPORT.md)). The decisive design fact maps onto Cammarata's "circuit ecology", where "circuits race to capture the remaining error, and the ones that reuse what's already there" grow fastest, so learning is "more about learning speed than storage capacity" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). Rewarded only by the answer's likelihood, **no seed learned the head fully, after about 1,750 flat steps in which the network memorized training pairs**. Adding a reward for the computer's result made the pointers settle in 250–1,000 steps in every seed ([CPUGPT_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_cpugpt/CPUGPT_REPORT.md)). Memorization reuses the embeddings already there and wins the race. The tool route needs discrete pointers learned by high-variance REINFORCE, and it wins only when rewarded directly. Zaremba & Sutskever's RL-NTM is the precedent for that split, with discrete interface decisions trained by REINFORCE and continuous parts by backprop ([RL-NTM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.00521)). Toolformer's rule of keeping a call only if its result helps is the self-supervised cousin of the result-check reward ([Toolformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761)).

Price changes the ecology the same way. At a call cost of 0.05 the best seed calls on **100% of math and 0% of non-math problems**. Two other seeds instead stop calling for multiplication, of which there are only about 1,400 legal pairs, and memorize the training pairs. They get about 20% on held-out ones ([CPUGPT_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_cpugpt/CPUGPT_REPORT.md)). Graves's ponder cost is the standard template for that trade-off ([ACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08983)). The static discipline also explains why the interface must be discrete. The CPU's 2-input gates tolerate input deviations of only 0.03–0.12, and noise at σ = 0.05 crashes every run within 53–225 ticks ([MINIGATES_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_minigates/MINIGATES_REPORT.md); [CPU_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_cpu/CPU_REPORT.md)). A soft attention mixture over operand addresses would feed it non-Boolean inputs. That last step is an inference: no soft-attention baseline was run.

The story GPT's call gate illustrates two more of the essay's claims. Cammarata argues that mixture-of-experts routing works against superposition because "the router is just one small linear layer" and cannot recognize thousands of packed features ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The project's call gate is that kind of router, and it is narrow. It makes **0 calls across 207,168 held-out story words containing 629 operation words**, yet lands its pointers right on 90.3–100% of held-out word problems ([STORYGPT_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_storygpt/STORYGPT_REPORT.md)). It also fails the essay's own falsification test. The explanation "fires on arithmetic word problems" predicts calls on new sentence shapes, but those score only 0–38%, and the report concludes that "the gate has learned the shapes rather than 'this is a word problem'" ([STORYGPT_REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_storygpt/STORYGPT_REPORT.md)). The narrower explanation wins. This matches the essay's observation that early generalization carries surface features ("mostly color, not shape") ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)), and shows that "explanations that could lose" works as a protocol even at 1.4M weights. The CPU head is also, by construction, the opposite of what Cammarata predicts for learned heads, which he expects to be "broad and recursively generalized" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). It is engineered rather than learned, so it does not test that prediction. Its one narrow, perfectly known job makes it a planted ground truth for any attention-interpretability method.

## Squeeze, watch and snap: a falsifiable test the repo can run now

The essay names its own decisive test and admits it cannot run it at scale. "Recursive generalization and superposition predict nearly opposite things, and you can watch a neuron grow and see which one happens". But "for something like a huge language model we don't have the checkpoints to watch it happen" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). It also concedes the domain where packing does occur: "memories that really are ends in themselves, related to nothing else. That's the toy's world, and there it does pack" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). The project's B-family adder can supply both missing pieces. It has a known algorithm, an exhaustively enumerable input space, and training cheap enough to checkpoint every 25 steps: a full B run of 2,500 steps took about 165 seconds on one thread ([B-seed0 training log](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/models/B-seed0.log)). The design borrows Cammarata's layer squeeze, his developmental test and his weak-activation ablation. It adds the project's margin scale and a positive control drawn from his conceded toy world. Following the project's practice and Leavitt & Morcos's call for "strongly falsifiable" interpretability ([Leavitt & Morcos 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12016)), every threshold below would be fixed in a timestamped `PREREG.md` before any model is trained ([Nosek et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1708274114); [PREREG template](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/PREREG.md)).

### Why width 27 is where the two accounts part

Every measurement happens at the query positions q_k for k = 1..9, as in the existing follow-up analysis of MLP units. A width-256 B model has 183–204 active units for about 27 jobs: three count thresholds at each of nine positions ([Follow-up SUMMARY](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_followup/SUMMARY.md)). Below 27 units the network cannot give every (threshold, position) pair its own neuron. It must do one of two things. It can **share one threshold across many positions**, which is generalization along the position axis and the analogue of the squeezed InceptionV1's "one 'curves & loops' neuron instead of ten curve detectors" ([Essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md)). Or it can **pack different thresholds at different positions onto one neuron**, using position-embedding biases. Both are realizable by a ReLU unit. Only one query position is active per token, so packed jobs never collide, which is exactly the sparsity under which Toy Models predicts superposition ([Elhage et al. 2022](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html)). There is also a sharp floor. By this report's own counting argument, not a project result: once the count arrives on one fused coordinate, parity of the count needs three kinks. So the smallest width that learns should, on Cammarata's account, show exactly three shared units: OR3, majority and AND3, or ramps with onsets at counts 1, 2 and 3. That outcome would also make the majority unit, the engineer's carry-out, unavoidable.

### Arms, labels and hypotheses

Arm A is the task under test. It uses the exact B recipe (1 layer, d_model 64, 4 heads, lowest-bit-first 10-bit addition, 2,500 steps, AdamW) at MLP widths 256, 32, 16, 8 and 4, with seeds 0–4. Arm R is the positive control from Cammarata's conceded world. It uses the same model and token format, but the target at position k is t_k = T_k(a_k, b_k, t_{k−1}), where each T_k is a *different* random balanced 3-input Boolean function. Those per-position jobs are unrelated by construction, so any neuron that serves two positions in arm R is packing. Arm 0 is every model at initialization. Each active unit u at each position k gets a label. That label is the best Boolean function of the position's three inputs if R² ≥ 0.8, the existing criterion. Otherwise it is a monotone "count ramp" with onset θ if the count explains η² ≥ 0.9, so graded ReLU units are not penalized for being analog. Otherwise it is "unclear". A unit counts as active at a position if it is non-zero on at least 5% of problems there. A unit then does **one job** if all its labelled positions share one label. It does **two related jobs** if they use two count thresholds one level apart (arm A only). It is **packed** for any other mix, and **unclear** if any active position is unlabelled. **Cleanliness** is the share doing one job or two related jobs, mirroring the essay's 90% plus 7%. **Consistency excess Δ** is the same-label rate over pairs of positions within a unit, minus its mean under 1,000 permutations of labels across units within each position.

### Controls that make a null result informative

Four validity gates come first, and failing any of them yields no verdict rather than a convenient one. **V1, reproducibility:** retraining width 256 with dense checkpoints must reproduce the saved B:0, B:2, B:3 and B:4 models, in exact accuracy and in the existing count-threshold tallies. **V2, sensitivity:** arm R must show a packed share ≥ 0.30 at widths ≤ 16 wherever it learns ≥ 95% per-bit accuracy. Otherwise the classifier cannot see packing and H3 is uninterpretable. **V3, specificity:** untrained models must score cleanliness ≤ 0.10 once single-input "dictator" labels are set aside. This exclusion is needed because random-init C2 models already contain 87–112 units that simply copy the query token b_k ([Transformer gates REPORT](file:///Users/alisongun/Downloads/xor_circuits%202/results_transformer_gates/REPORT.md)). **V4, privileged basis:** multiplying the post-ReLU activations by random orthogonal matrices (10 draws) must cut arm A's cleanliness by at least half. If it does not, cleanliness is not a property of neurons and H1–H3 are moot. This rotation null follows Gurnee et al.'s random-baseline logic ([Gurnee et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12181)). Two smaller controls complete the design. Planted synthetic units in `tests/test_circuits.py` (an OR3 at k = 2 that is an AND3 at k = 7 must classify as packed, and a majority shared across all positions as one job) check the classifier before it touches real models. Wilson intervals on every accuracy keep differences between widths honest. Because the snaps are replacement interventions on the clean run, they are reported as such, as Heimersheim & Nanda recommend ([Heimersheim & Nanda](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15255)).

### How each outcome moves the debate

If H1–H3 pass with V2 passing, Cammarata's developmental falsifier has been run with ground truth for the first time. Structured computation generalized outward from one origin. Unrelated content packed, as he concedes it should. That is a clean demonstration that "the question is which world real networks are in" has an operational answer. If arm A packs under the squeeze while arm R validates the classifier, the project has produced what the essay says would make the author "update strongly": a convincing inexplicable group, in a structured task. Because B:0–B:4 share architecture, size and optimizer, it would be hard to dismiss as bad learning. The fair reading would be narrower than "superposition wins", though. It would be that the "last hypothesis" ordering needs a capacity condition, which is Liv Gorton's point that superposition means holding "more ideas than neurons" ([Implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/goodfire-cammarata-ai-neurons-easier-to-read/)). If H1 alone fails, with B:3 packing at full width, that is the strongest single result available, since neither squeeze nor size can explain it. H4 decides something orthogonal. A count snap that holds while a two-level snap fails would show the network's neurons are gates in Cammarata's semantic sense but not in the project's electrical sense. Weak activations that turn out to matter in exact arithmetic, against ConvNeXt's 72%-to-71%, would show the essay's weak-activation result depends on the task. The experiment cannot reach the essay's Qwen3-32B or Kimi K3 numbers. Every result here lives in what Cammarata would call the toy's world. Its value is that the toy's world is the only place where "clean", "packed" and "grew from one origin" can be checked against a known answer.

## Conclusion

The project's own arc is the strongest available evidence that Cammarata's "bad explanations" cause is real. A pre-registered study looked for the engineer's noun, a carry neuron, and failed nine of its predictions by the scorecard's count. Then it found that the network's own variable, a thresholded count, accounts for every clean MLP unit in four seeds and makes the failed transplant succeed. The essay makes the same correction from cat/car to V-shape. What the project adds, and the essay's percentages cannot see, is a second axis of cleanliness. A neuron can have one main job and still be an analog ramp that would not survive a deep chain without borrowed restoration. The margin scale the project already uses (0.5, about 3, 104) is a ready-made column for any neuron audit, including Goodfire's.

The practical implication is that the essay's most important open question has become an afternoon's computation rather than a matter of interpretation. Cammarata frames the dispute with his critics as a question about which world networks live in. The squeeze-and-watch design answers it in a world with full checkpoints, a positive control he has already conceded, and one seed, B:3, that already looks like an exception. Whichever way B:3 falls, the result is publishable. It either confirms recursive generalization along the position axis of a network that resisted every earlier tool, or it supplies the first non-local packing example in a task with real structure. The natural next study after this one is to point the same classifier at the readers of the frozen CPU head, to ask whether the neurons that learn to read a perfectly clean component become clean themselves.
