Story GPT with a Gate CPU

A small GPT trained on children's stories. One attention head in its last layer is a frozen 8-bit computer built from trained neuron gates. The model writes the story itself, and when a sum comes up it points the computer at the numbers and the operation, runs it, and writes the computer's answer. Marked words came from the computer.
