Story GPT that calls a Gate CPU

A small GPT trained on children's stories. When a sum comes up it pulls out a calculator: it types 12 + 5 = into the story itself, an 8-bit computer built from trained neuron gates runs the sum and types 17 back into the text, and the GPT reads that and carries on: “now he has 17 apples”. The computer's answer is only ever read from the page, never pushed into the GPT's output, so the GPT can use it again for a second step.
